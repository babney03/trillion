# Surface brief: Keelson landing page + app shell

Scope: the product landing page (Persuade) and the app it opens (Operate) share one world. Audience: owners/skippers of small Canadian commercial vessels, on phones at the dock, bright light, patchy signal, many older. Action: run the free class finder, see their deadline, start a draft SMS for their boat. Proof available: the regulation itself, TC registry counts, the working product. No customers or testimonials exist — none may be shown.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The SMS is drawn like the vessel's own fire-and-safety plan: a naval architect's general-arrangement sheet with title block, revision table and ISO safety symbols. Refuses the category default of navy-and-white SaaS with wave graphics and a life ring.

OWN-WORLD: Drafting-film white ground, ink-black 1px linework, dimension lines and station ticks; colour only where safety plans use it — fire red, lifesaving green, a single caution yellow for due items. Title-block cells, revision table, callout balloons with leader lines. Archivo for lettering, tabular numerals for every date.

STORY: A skipper finds their class and deadline in seconds, sees their own boat drawn up with its hazards called out, understands Keelson builds and keeps the manual and the records, and starts a free draft.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline and class finder (three questions, instant answer with deadline countdown). Right, a vessel profile drawing with callout balloons naming hazards and records, framed by a title block. Primary action under the finder.

FORM: Ship's general-arrangement / safety-plan drawing — position 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 690d0cd2.
