# Outreach — round two

Three concept sites, three messages, built from the pitch kit's structure at
the $800 starting price. None of these businesses publishes an email address
in any listing I found, so each one says how to reach them instead.

**Before sending any of them:**

1. Put the site live (drag the folder from `deploy/` onto
   https://app.netlify.com/drop) and paste that URL in place of `[LINK]`.
   Open it on your own phone first.
2. Replace `[Your name]` and `[Phone]`.
3. Re-read the first two sentences in your own voice and change anything that
   doesn't sound like you.
4. CASL: a one-off, relevant message to a business's published contact channel
   is generally fine. Always say who you are, give a way to reach you, and
   honour "no thanks" immediately.
