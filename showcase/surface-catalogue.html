What I can and can't do

Straight answer

Yes

Design

Full pages, built in code. Layout, type, colour, motion, responsive down to a phone, light and dark.

Surfaces

Backgrounds and textures like the twelve above, retinted per client, plus any Canva or Higgsfield file you hand me.

Content

Slots for their stuff. Photos, reviews, hours, service list — you send them, I place them and adjust the fit.

Found

Local SEO groundwork. Business schema, page titles, service-area markup — what makes them show up in map results.

No

Tools

No Canva or Higgsfield access from this environment. I can't log in or export from them — only take what you export.

Photos

I don't produce imagery. Which matches your plan: real photos from the real business always sell better than generated ones.

Server

No backend by default. Forms need a service wiring up — a few minutes, but it's a step, and usually a small monthly cost.

Live

I can't buy domains or hosting. I can hand you a finished site and tell you exactly where to put it.
